My Top Four Personal Values

1. Stability

Stability allows a person to feel secure and confident in all aspects of their life. I've learned this value mainly from my family, teachers, and personal experiences. Stability is important because it ensures a sense of confidence to continue your day to day activities without worries. Without stability, there could be severe repercussions to ones mental health leading to anxiety, stress, and even depression.

2. Success

Success to me means acheiving your desired goals and having the chance reach new heights. Personally, this value comes from school, work experiences, and family expectations. Success is important because it improves your life and helps you reach your full potential. Failing to succeed can cause a person to hesitate to take risks and miss opportunities of growth.

3. Respect

Respect involves treating others fairly and valuing different perspectives. I learned this value through interactions with family members, friends, and diverse communities. Respect is important because it promotes cooperation and positive relationships. Without respect, conflicts increase and teamwork becomes difficult.

4. Wisdom

Wisdom is the experiences and knowledge gained throughout different aspects of life.This value comes from my personal experiences, education, and people I look up to. It is important because with wisdom, you gain empathy and a clear understanding of the world around you. Without wisdom,people tend to make poor decisions that can lead to negative outcomes.
